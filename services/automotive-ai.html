Automotive Mechanic AI & Systems FAQs

Which automotive mechanic AI and workshop systems do you set up and run for London garages?

We set up, network, and manage leading diagnostic and workshop platforms including Autodata, HaynesPro WorkshopData, Bosch ESI[tronic], Snap-on Intelligent Diagnostics, ALLDATA Repair, Opus IVS, Garage Hive, TechMan, BOOKAR GMS, Tekmetric, WorkshopEase, and MechIQ across Greater London.

How do you stop workshop diagnostic tablets and J2534 ECU tools from dropping offline in metal bays?

We install commercial Ethernet cabling and ruggedised workshop Wi-Fi access points segregated onto dedicated garage Local Area Networks (LANs), keeping Snap-on, Bosch KTS, Autel, Launch, and J2534 pass-through flashing laptops reliably connected without interference from guest Wi-Fi.

Do you handle Automatic Number Plate Recognition (ANPR), DVLA/MOT integration, and invoice scanning automation?

Yes. We configure reception ANPR bay cameras, DVLA Vehicle Registration Mark (VRM) lookups, GOV.UK MOT expiry reminders, Optical Character Recognition (OCR) supplier invoice scanning, Digital Vehicle Health Checks (VHC), and accounting sync with Xero, QuickBooks, or Sage.

Can you also build and run custom automotive AI or bespoke garage systems?

Yes. When off-the-shelf software does not match your specialist vehicles or workflow, we build, host, and manage custom systems—including bespoke API bridges between diagnostic tools and billing, private workshop AI assistants, and custom dashboards.
