Ring Doorbell Installation FAQs

Do you supply mounting, wiring, and app setup for Ring Video Doorbells in London?

Yes. From our Hampstead base, we attend homes and flats across Greater London to fit battery or wired Ring doorbells cleanly on brick, render, uPVC, or timber frames, pair chimes, and configure motion zones.

My Ring doorbell already exists but lags or disconnects—can you fix it?

Yes. Slow live view and missed rings are nearly always caused by weak porch Wi-Fi signals. We measure front-door signal quality and position a Chime Pro or proper mesh node to fix delays.

Can you advise if I want to switch away from monthly Ring fees?

Yes—if you would prefer zero recurring video storage charges, ask about our Subscription-Free Smart Doorbells service storing footage privately at home.
