Restaurant Till & Order System FAQs

What does a London restaurant till and kitchen order installation include?

We configure your front-of-house POS till, load full food and drink menus with modifiers and allergens, wire Ethernet or Wi-Fi kitchen ticket printers and Kitchen Display Systems (KDS), set up table plans and split-bill rules, and train your staff before opening.

Can you fix missed kitchen tickets and unreliable takeaway tablet Wi-Fi?

Yes. Missed tickets are usually caused by consumer-grade routers, Wi-Fi dead zones behind stainless-steel kitchen walls, or changing IP addresses. We assign static Local Area Network (LAN) addresses and install wired Ethernet or commercial access points across London venues.

Do you install hospitality POS across all of London or only Hampstead?

We are based in Hampstead and install restaurant, café, pub, and takeaway till systems across all of Greater London, including Central London, Soho, Shoreditch, Camden, Islington, Westminster, Kensington, and South London.
