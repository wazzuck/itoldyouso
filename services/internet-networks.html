London Wi-Fi & Network FAQs

How do you fix Wi-Fi dead zones in thick-walled Victorian London properties?

Instead of relying on cheap plug-in extenders that halve speed, we survey signal walls, wire discreet Cat6 Ethernet backhaul where practical, and deploy business-grade multi-point Wi-Fi.

Can you separate commercial till networks from public customer Wi-Fi?

Yes. For cafés, shops, restaurants, salons, and offices across London, we segment Point of Sale (POS) tills and card machines onto dedicated VLANs with optional 4G/5G cellular failover.

Do you install network switches and patch tidy-ups across Greater London?

Yes. From our Hampstead base, we attend residential townhouses, flats, and multi-floor shops or offices across all London boroughs.
