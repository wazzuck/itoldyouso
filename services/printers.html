London Printer & Till Receipt Setup FAQs

Which home, office, and till printers do you install and fix?

We install wireless laser and inkjet multifunction printers (HP, Brother, Canon, Epson), Apple AirPrint scanners, and commercial Epson TM-series or Star Micronics thermal POS receipt and kitchen printers.

Why do Wi-Fi printers keep showing 'Offline' in London homes and offices?

Routers routinely reassign Dynamic Host Configuration Protocol (DHCP) IP addresses or separate 2.4GHz and 5GHz bands. We reserve clean static IP addresses and configure stable driver ports so printers stay online.

Can you configure scan-to-folder and scan-to-email on office printers?

Yes. We configure modern OAuth/SMTP scan-to-email and secure network scan folders across Windows, macOS, Microsoft 365, and Google Workspace.
