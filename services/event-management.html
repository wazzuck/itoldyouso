Event Management Technology FAQs

What ticket barcode and scanning technologies do you set up for London events?

We supply and configure 1D Code 128 and EAN barcodes, 2D QR Code, PDF417 and Aztec tickets, handheld and hands-free readers from Zebra, Datalogic and Honeywell, and smartphone camera scanning apps over wired, Bluetooth, and Wi-Fi links—with duplicate detection, revocation lists, and encrypted rotating QR codes reprinted the morning of the event.

Can ticket scanning and check-in keep working if the venue internet drops?

Yes. We design local-first check-in: encrypted guest lists cached on each scanner, validation against a server on your own network or a synchronised list shared between doors, and automatic reconciliation when connectivity returns, so a broadband failure never stops a queue at the gate.

Do you provide RFID wristbands, cashless payments, and access control gates?

Yes. We deploy Near Field Communication (NFC) and Radio-Frequency Identification (RFID) wristbands for cashless top-ups and tiered access, turnstiles and scanning pedestals with zone permissions, badge and wristband printers for accreditation, and roaming card readers for bars and merchandise stalls, with clear refunds of any unused balance after the event.
