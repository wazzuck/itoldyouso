Telephony & Virtual Landline FAQs

How do virtual landline numbers work, and can I keep my existing 020 London number?

A virtual landline works over the internet rather than a physical copper socket, letting you make and receive calls on an 020 London, UK regional, 0800, or 0330 number from iOS or Android mobile apps, laptops, or desk phones anywhere. We can also port your existing landline number onto modern cloud telephony with zero lost calls.

What does the UK PSTN and ISDN landline switchover mean for my business or home?

Openreach is retiring legacy copper Public Switched Telephone Network (PSTN) and Integrated Services Digital Network (ISDN) lines across the United Kingdom (UK), moving all voice traffic onto internet-based digital connections. We audit every line on site—including main numbers, lift lines, door intercoms, alarms, and card terminals—and migrate you cleanly onto Fibre to the Premises (FTTP) or Single Order Generic Ethernet Access (SoGEA) digital voice.

Which VoIP platforms, Cloud PBX systems, and IP handsets do you set up and manage?

We deploy and administer Microsoft Teams Phone, Zoom Phone, 3CX, RingCentral, Vonage, 8x8, Dialpad, bOnline, Yay.com, and Gamma Horizon, paired with Yealink, Poly, Cisco, Grandstream, Snom, and Gigaset Internet Protocol (IP) desk phones, Digital Enhanced Cordless Telecommunications (DECT) handsets, and Analogue Telephone Adapters (ATAs) for cherished vintage telephones.

Can you link incoming phone calls to our Point of Sale (POS) till, booking system, or AI receptionist?

Yes. We configure caller-ID screen-pops into Customer Relationship Management (CRM), hospitality, takeaway till, and clinic booking systems, alongside Interactive Voice Response (IVR) menus, voicemail-to-email, call recording, and Artificial Intelligence (AI) voice receptionists that answer or summarise calls out of hours.
