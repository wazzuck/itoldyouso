Card Payment Terminal FAQs

Which card machines and UK payment providers do you set up in London?

We configure SumUp, Square, Zettle by PayPal, Stripe Terminal, Barclaycard Anywhere, Lloyds Cardnet, Worldpay, Clover, Lightspeed, and Shopify POS card readers across London-wide premises.

Why do contactless card readers keep disconnecting in busy London venues?

Payment terminals often share congested guest Wi-Fi, suffer from Bluetooth drop-offs, or sit behind double NAT routers. We place tills and card machines on dedicated Ethernet or segmented Wi-Fi with optional 4G cellular failover.

Can you connect my till and card payouts to Xero, QuickBooks, or Sage?

Yes. We configure automated daily settlement and VAT summary feeds from Square, SumUp, Zettle, Lightspeed, and Shopify POS directly into Xero, QuickBooks, or Sage.

Do you visit areas outside Hampstead for card terminal setups?

Yes. While we are based in Hampstead, we attend commercial sites across all of Greater London, including Central London, Soho, Shoreditch, Westminster, the City of London, Camden, Hackney, Kensington, and South London.
