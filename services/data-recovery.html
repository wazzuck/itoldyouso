London Data Recovery FAQs

Can you recover files from a laptop that will no longer boot?

Yes. In most cases, even when Windows or macOS refuses to start, the underlying drive can be imaged safely using read-only recovery workflows so documents, emails, and photos are rescued intact.

Do you handle failed USB sticks, SD cards, and external hard drives?

Yes. We recover deleted or corrupted partitions and failing external drives—while advising immediately to power off clicking or physically damaged disks to prevent further sector loss.
