London Home Automation FAQs

Which smart home platforms do you install and configure across London?

We work with Apple HomeKit, Home Assistant, Philips Hue, Lutron, Tado, Nest, Sonos, and smart relays, prioritising reliable local control rather than fragile cloud-only gadgets.

Why do smart bulbs and switches keep going 'No Response' in London homes?

Consumer ISP routers often struggle with dozens of IoT clients or drop multicast (mDNS) traffic between bands. We tune home Wi-Fi cleanly so smart heating, blinds, and lights respond reliably every time.

Can I avoid monthly smart-home subscriptions?

Yes. We design automations, scenes, and local sensors that run locally inside your home without locking basic features behind ongoing monthly contracts.
