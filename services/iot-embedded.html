IoT & Embedded Devices FAQs

What types of IoT and embedded device projects do you build and support in London?

We design, programme, and maintain Raspberry Pi, ESP32, Arduino, STM32, and industrial Single-Board Computer (SBC) deployments—ranging from cold-chain fridge temperature logging for hospitality venues and air-quality sensors in offices to custom Modbus equipment bridges and bespoke hardware prototypes.

Can you build IoT systems that work locally without monthly cloud subscriptions?

Yes. We specialise in local-first architectures using Message Queuing Telemetry Transport (MQTT), ESPHome, Home Assistant, InfluxDB, and Grafana running on your own server or Raspberry Pi so your sensors and controllers keep working even if vendor clouds shut down or your internet drops.

Do you write custom firmware and connect industrial buses such as Modbus, CAN, SPI, or I2C?

Yes. We write and debug C, C++, MicroPython, and Embedded Linux software communicating over Modbus RTU/TCP, Recommended Standard 485 (RS-485), Controller Area Network (CAN bus), Serial Peripheral Interface (SPI), Inter-Integrated Circuit (I2C), and Universal Asynchronous Receiver-Transmitter (UART) serial links.

How do you stop smart devices and IoT sensors from creating cyber security risks on our network?

We isolate Internet of Things (IoT) hardware onto dedicated Virtual Local Area Networks (VLANs) with strict firewall rules, disable insecure legacy protocols, enforce encrypted Transport Layer Security (TLS) telemetry where supported, and set up clean Over-The-Air (OTA) firmware updates.
