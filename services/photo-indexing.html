Photo Indexing & Organisation FAQs

How do you turn thousands of disorganised photos into a searchable archive?

We gather scattered files from old phones, laptops, USB sticks, iCloud, and Google Photos, deduplicate exact copies, fix timestamps, and index them with private Artificial Intelligence (AI) search by face, place, and object.

Do my private family photos have to be uploaded to a third-party AI cloud?

No. Where desired, we run local private indexing on your Mac, Windows computer, or home Network Attached Storage (NAS) server so your family photos stay strictly under your roof.

How are large multi-drive photo organisation jobs priced?

After a free initial consultation to estimate photo volume and storage locations, IToldYouSo Computing gives a fixed, up-front statement-of-work price before starting.
