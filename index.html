Where are you based and which areas of London do you cover?

We are based in Hampstead (North West London) and serve commercial and residential sites across all of Greater London, including Central London, Camden, Westminster, City of London, Hackney, Islington, Barnet, Kensington and Chelsea, West London, East London, and South London, plus remote support nationwide.

Do you install Point of Sale (POS) and till systems across London?

Yes. We provide London-wide on-site till and Electronic Point of Sale (EPOS) installation for cafes, restaurants, takeaways, retail shops, salons, and market stalls. We configure Square, SumUp, Zettle by PayPal, Clover, Lightspeed, Shopify POS, Stripe Terminal, thermal receipt printers, cash drawers, barcode scanners, and Kitchen Display Systems (KDS), including pre-opening and out-of-hours cutovers.

Can you integrate card payment machines and audit processing fees?

Yes. We install and connect card readers from Square, SumUp, Zettle, Barclaycard, Worldpay, Lloyds Cardnet, and Stripe Terminal over reliable Wi-Fi or Ethernet and link sales to Xero, QuickBooks, or Sage. For independent United Kingdom (UK) card acquirer fee audits and three-year Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) modelling, we also partner with our sister practice TillSwift Payment Consultation (tillswift.com).

How quickly can you attend for urgent till issues or emergency IT callouts?

We provide often same-day follow-up and always respond within 24 hours across London, prioritising urgent Point of Sale (POS) outages, kitchen printer failures, network drops, and critical hardware or security issues.

Do you assist domestic households as well as commercial businesses?

Yes. Alongside commercial till setups, office networks, servers, cyber security testing, and custom software for London businesses, we support private households with Wi-Fi dead zones, computer repairs, data recovery, smart TVs, digital lockouts, and subscription-free smart doorbells.

How do you charge for POS installations and IT services?

Every enquiry starts with a free initial telephone consultation. Standard engineering, troubleshooting, and training are billed at £50 per hour, with fixed-price project packages quoted upfront for turnkey London till installations, ChromeOS Flex migrations, websites, and bespoke software.
