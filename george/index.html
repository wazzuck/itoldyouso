1. What Your Till Does

Your Square Register is a modern Point of Sale (POS) system. In plain English, that means it is a computerised till designed to handle all your daily sales cleanly and reliably:

  • It adds up your customer's purchases so you never have to do mental arithmetic under pressure.
  • It accepts card payments (contactless tap, chip and PIN, Apple Pay, and Google Pay).
  • It records cash payments and works out the exact change to hand back to the customer.
  • It prints tidy paper receipts or sends receipts by text message or email.
  • It automatically totals up all your daily takings, saving you hours of paperwork at the end of each trading day.

2. The Parts of Your Till

Your till consists of a few simple, high-quality components:

  • The Main Screen (facing you): This is your control centre. You touch the screen to type amounts, select items, and see what the customer owes.
  • The Customer Screen (facing the buyer): This smaller screen shows the customer the total bill and guides them through tapping or inserting their card.
  • The Card Reader Slot & Tap Zone: Built straight into the customer screen. The top edge is for tapping contactless cards or phones; the bottom slot is for inserting chip cards.
  • The Receipt Printer: A compact box loaded with thermal paper that prints receipts without messy ink cartridges.
  • The Cash Drawer: Connected directly to the till. It clicks open automatically whenever you complete a cash sale.
  • The Power Cables: Plugs neatly into your mains wall socket to keep the till powered and ready throughout the day.

3. Turning It On and Connecting to the Internet

Turning On the Till

  1. Ensure the mains power plug at the wall is switched on.
  2. Locate the discreet power button on the back or edge of the main screen.
  3. Press and hold the power button for 2 seconds until the screen lights up with the Square logo.
  4. Wait approximately 30 seconds while the system loads your checkout screen.

Checking the Internet Connection

Look at the very top right-hand corner of your screen:

  • A white Wi-Fi fan symbol (curved bars) or an Ethernet symbol means your till is connected to the internet and ready to trade.
  • If you see a grey symbol or an exclamation mark, the till is temporarily disconnected. Check your shop broadband router or see Section 9 for simple fixes.

4. Taking a Payment — Step by Step

Taking a card payment takes just a few calm seconds. Follow these steps:

  1. Enter the amount: Type the price using the keypad (for example, type 1 5 0 0 for £15.00) or tap the item tile on your screen.
  2. Check your screen: Glance at the top right of your screen to confirm the total matches what you intend to charge.
  3. Tap the blue Charge button: Tap the big blue button that says Charge £15.00.
  4. Customer pays: The customer screen will light up with the amount. The customer can pay in one of three easy ways:
    • Tap (Contactless): They hold their card, smartphone, or smartwatch against the top edge of the customer screen.
    • Insert (Chip and PIN): They push their card into the bottom slot with the gold chip facing up, then type their secret 4-digit Personal Identification Number (PIN) on the customer screen.
    • Swipe: For older or international cards without a chip, they slide the magnetic stripe through the side slot.
  5. Listen for the chime: When the payment is accepted, you will hear a cheerful chime and see a big green tick on your screen. That means the money is safely in the bank!
  6. Receipt: Ask the customer: "Would you like a receipt?" If they say yes, tap Print Receipt. If they say no, tap No Receipt.
  7. Ready for next: Tap New Sale to clear the screen for your next customer.

5. Taking a Cash Payment

The till handles all the change arithmetic so you never need to calculate figures in your head:

  1. Type the amount or choose the item on your keypad as normal.
  2. Tap the blue Charge £15.00 button.
  3. On the payment screen, tap the Cash button.
  4. Tap the note button the customer handed you (such as £10 or £20), or type the exact amount of cash given.
  5. The screen will instantly display in big bold numbers the Change Due, and your cash drawer will click open.
  6. Count out the change, hand it to the customer, push the cash drawer firmly shut until it clicks, and tap Done.

6. Giving a Refund

If a customer returns an item or an error was entered, giving a refund is straightforward:

  1. Tap the menu button or tap Transactions (the little clock icon at the bottom of the screen).
  2. Find the sale from the list (you can identify it by the time or the amount) and tap it.
  3. Tap the button that says Issue Refund.
  4. Select the items or type the refund amount, then tap Next.
  5. Select the reason (such as "Returned Goods" or "Incorrect Amount"), then tap Refund.
  6. The customer screen will invite the customer to tap or insert the card they used to buy the item. The money is automatically returned to their account.

Your till has built-in features to make text and numbers clear and easy on the eyes:

If any number or text looks small, simply tap the screen 3 times quickly with one finger. The screen will instantly zoom in large. You can drag two fingers across the glass to slide around. When you are finished, tap 3 times quickly again to return to normal size.

9. When Something Goes Wrong

If something unexpected happens, remain calm. Follow these simple numbered fixes:

1. Internet Dropped Out

  1. Do not worry. Your till has an automatic Offline Mode that lets you continue taking card payments even if broadband is temporarily disconnected.
  2. Look at your shop internet router on the wall to check that the power light is green.
  3. Switch the router off at the wall socket, count slowly to 30, and switch it back on.
  4. Once the broadband reconnects, the till will automatically process any queued payments.

2. The Screen Is Frozen

  1. Sales data is saved safely in the cloud, so you will not lose anything.
  2. Press and hold the power button on the back of the screen firmly for 10 full seconds until the screen turns completely black.
  3. Wait 5 seconds, then press the power button once to turn it back on. The till will reboot cleanly.

3. A Customer's Card Won't Go Through

  1. Ask the customer to gently wipe the gold chip on their sleeve and insert it once more.
  2. Suggest they try tapping the card contactlessly, or using Apple Pay / Google Pay on their phone.
  3. If the card still declines, the customer's bank has declined the transaction. Politely say: "The machine says this card was declined by your bank. Would you like to try another card or cash?"

4. The Receipt Printer Won't Print

  1. Check if the paper has run out (a pink stripe along the paper means the roll is nearly empty).
  2. Open the printer latch.
  3. Ensure the paper feeds from underneath the roll (not over the top). Thermal paper only prints on one side!
  4. Pull 2 inches of paper out past the cutter and push the lid down firmly until it clicks.

5. Something Else Unfamiliar

  1. Pick up your mobile phone and take a clear photograph of what is showing on the screen.
  2. Note down the approximate time.
  3. Ring me directly on 020 4520 9114. A photo lets me see exactly what you are seeing!

13. Glossary of Terms

  • Point of Sale (POS): The modern name for a computerised shop till.
  • Contactless (Tap): Paying by touching a card, phone, or smartwatch near the screen without entering a PIN.
  • Chip and PIN: Paying by slotting a card into the reader and typing a 4-digit secret number.
  • Receipt: A paper slip or digital message showing proof of purchase.
  • Refund: Returning money directly to a customer's card when an item is returned.
  • Square Dashboard: The website you can open on your home computer to view sales totals.
  • Offline Mode: A safety feature that lets the till store card payments even if broadband briefly fails.
