CAFE & BRUNCH SPOT TILL — COUNTERTOP QUICK CHEAT SHEET

Keep this by the till • My number: 020 4520 9114

1. Take a Card Payment

  1. Tap drinks/food or enter amount.
  2. Tap modifiers (e.g. oat milk, extra shot).
  3. Tap green Charge / Pay button.
  4. Select Card payment.
  5. Customer taps card/phone or inserts chip.
  6. Wait for green tick & confirmation chime.
  7. Ask if customer wants a printed receipt.
  8. Tap Finish Sale for next order.

2. Take a Cash Payment

  1. Tap order items and tap Charge / Pay.
  2. Select Cash.
  3. Tap note handed to you (e.g. £20).
  4. Cash drawer pops open automatically.
  5. Screen shows change to give back.
  6. Hand change to customer & close drawer firmly.

3. End-of-Day Cashing Up

  1. Tap Reports → Close Register.
  2. Count cash drawer notes and coins.
  3. Type cash counted and tap Close Register.
  4. Tap Print Z-Report Summary.
  5. Clip card machine summary to the paper slip.

4. Give a Refund or Void

  1. Tap Menu → Transactions.
  2. Find order by time, amount, or card digits.
  3. Tap Issue Refund.
  4. Choose items or enter partial amount.
  5. Tap Refund to Card (or Cash).
  6. Hand printed refund receipt to customer.

5. Something Went Wrong?

  • Screen frozen: Hold power button on back for 10 seconds to reboot. No sales data is lost.
  • Card reader disconnected: Check Wi-Fi/Bluetooth icon in top bar is ON.
  • Drawer won't pop: Check telephone wire behind receipt printer is plugged tight.
  • Receipt won't feed: Open printer lid and make sure paper feeds from under the roll.
