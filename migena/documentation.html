Owner tasks: do these 3 in order

Only the owner does these tasks. Work through one card at a time. Task 1 must be ticked before you start tasks 2 and 3. Task 2 must be ticked before you start task 3. If a step does not match what you see, stop and ring Neville Bryce on 020 4520 9114. Do not guess.

Owner task 1 of 3 Do this first Owner only Do it yourself

Verify your identity

When: ASAP

You need: your own mobile phone, your own SumUp login, and a valid photo ID such as a passport or a photo driving licence.

Follow these steps in order

  1. Sign in to your own SumUp profile, in a web browser or in the SumUp app on your own phone.
  2. Look at the top of the screen for the verification banner. Tap it.
  3. If the form asks for them, fill in your details exactly as they appear on your ID.
  4. When asked, scan your photo ID.
  5. When asked, take a selfie and submit it.
  6. Submit everything, then wait for SumUp's message.

Rules: you do this yourself. Do not let staff or the engineer do it for you, and do not use anyone else's login or phone. Do not send a photo of your ID to anyone by text or e-mail, including Neville. Scan it only on SumUp's own screens.

Stop and ring Neville on 020 4520 9114 if you cannot find the verification banner, the scan keeps failing, or SumUp asks for something you do not understand.

This is owner task 1 of 3. It is separate from the other tasks. Verifying your identity does not update the software, turn on cash payments or set up the printers.

Owner task 2 of 3 Owner only

Update the software

Start only after task 1 is ticked.

When: before you open on Sunday 22 November 2026, and again on the morning of that day.

Why: if the devices run different versions, orders can show different statuses on different devices.

Follow these steps in order

  1. Make sure no customer is waiting and no sale is on the register screen.
  2. On the register, tap More or Settings. If you cannot see either, stop and ring Neville. Then look for an update option. If an update is shown, tap it and follow the screens.
  3. Do the same on the card reader (SumUp Solo). If the reader shows an update, follow the screens.
  4. Do the same on the kitchen Terminal.
  5. Do not switch anything off while an update is running. Wait until each update has finished.
  6. Switch off each device, wait 10 seconds, then switch it on again. Wait until each screen is ready.
  7. On each device, find the app version number. Check that all three numbers are the same.
Stop and ring Neville on 020 4520 9114 if an update fails, a device will not restart, the three version numbers differ, or you cannot find where to check the version.

This is owner task 2 of 3. It is separate from the other tasks. Do not start task 3 until this box is ticked.

Owner task 3 of 3 Owner only

Turn on cash payments

Start only after task 2 is ticked.

Why: until this is on, the till takes card payments only.

Follow these steps in order

  1. Make sure no customer is waiting and no sale is on the register screen.
  2. Look at the bottom-left corner of the screen. Tap the small round profile icon.
  3. In the menu that opens, tap Cash payments.
  4. Turn on the switch beside Accept cash payments. If it is already on, leave it on.
  5. Test it: start a new sale with one cheap item. Tap the button that takes payment, often labelled Charge or Pay.
  6. Look for Cash in the list of ways to pay. You should see it.
  7. Do not tap Cash and do not take any money. Leave the test sale without paying. If you are not sure how, ring Neville.
Stop and ring Neville on 020 4520 9114 if you cannot find the profile icon, there is no Cash payments option, or the switch will not stay on.

Neville Bryce owns these six tasks, so you do not need to do them. He will tell you when each one is done. Where he needs a decision from you, he will ask. If you notice a printer, Wi-Fi or till problem before he has finished, ring him on 020 4520 9114.

Neville task 1 of 6 Owned by Neville

Route drinks to the bar printer and food to the kitchen printer

Start only after the owner has ticked owner task 2.

Why: so drink orders print at the bar and food orders print in the kitchen.

Neville checks these steps in order

  1. Check that the register, the card reader and the kitchen Terminal are on the same software version.
  2. On each device that holds a printer, open the printer settings. If they cannot be found, check the software versions again before going on.
  3. Look for Select categories on each printer. If it is missing, stop. Agree another way to send food and drink orders to the right place with the owner before going on.
  4. On the kitchen printer, select only the food categories, 01 to 11. Save the settings.
  5. On the bar printer, select only the drink categories, 12 to 18 and 20 to 22. Do not select 19 Drink Extras, which is empty. Save the settings.
  6. Test: park one order with a food item and one drink. The bar printer should print only the drink, and the kitchen printer only the food.
Stop if a device will not restart, the printer settings cannot be found, or the software versions do not match. Do not change the routing until they match.

Status: to do, on site. The owner does not need to do this task.

Neville task 2 of 6 Owned by Neville Not a blocker

Check the Wi-Fi for dropouts

Start only after the owner has ticked owner task 2.

Why: if the till, card reader or kitchen Terminal lose Wi-Fi during service, payments can fail and orders can stop printing. It is not a blocker to opening, but it can affect business if it is not spotted early.

Neville checks these steps in order

  1. Stand at the till, at the card reader's usual position and at the kitchen Terminal's usual position. Check the Wi-Fi symbol on each screen.
  2. If any device shows one bar, a cross or No connection, note the exact spot.
  3. With each device in its normal place, start one test sale and send it to the printers.
  4. Watch the screen during the sale. If a spinner stays for more than 10 seconds, or a printer does not respond, note it.
  5. Repeat at a quiet time and again during a busy period if possible, because dropouts often show up when the network is under load.
  6. Record when each dropout happens and which device is affected.
Stop and investigate if a device keeps losing connection, a payment fails because of the network, or a printer stops responding mid-service. Tell the owner what you found.

Status: to do, on site. The owner should ring Neville about any dropout they see.

Neville task 3 of 6 Owned by Neville

Write down how to print the bill before payment

Why: so the customer can check the bill on paper before paying.

Neville to do

  1. Find how to print the bill from the till before payment, and check which printer prints it.
  2. Confirm the steps on the till, using a test order and without taking payment. Then write them into the Till Procedures & Features guide in plain steps.
  3. Check that the printed bill shows each item, its modifiers and the prices, so the customer can check it.

Status: to do. The owner does not need to do this task.

Neville task 4 of 6 Owned by Neville

Print each modifier under its food or drink item on tickets

Why: at the moment each modifier, such as oat milk or an extra shot, prints as a separate item. The kitchen and bar need to see it under the item it belongs to.

Neville to do

  1. Check the ticket and modifier settings on each device that prints tickets, and find the setting that prints a modifier under its item.
  2. If the till cannot do this, ask SumUp support, and tell the owner before changing the menu.
  3. Test with a mixed order: a drink with a modifier, such as oat milk, and a food item. Each modifier should print under its item on the right ticket.

Status: to do. The owner does not need to do this task.

Neville task 5 of 6 Owned by Neville

Print the name of the member of staff on each order ticket

Why: the kitchen has asked for it, so that any query from the kitchen or bar can go to the right person.

Neville to do

  1. Check whether the order tickets can show the name of the member of staff who placed the order.
  2. If they can, check what the name is taken from. It may need each person to sign in with their own Personal Identification Number (PIN). PIN login was off when the till was checked on 19 November 2026.
  3. If the tickets cannot show the name, tell the owner before deciding what to do.

Status: to do. The owner does not need to do this task.

Neville task 6 of 6 Owned by Neville

Add a payout method so the owner can withdraw cash from SumUp

Why: so the owner can take the takings out of the SumUp account as cash.

Neville to do

  1. Ask the owner which withdrawal method they want. The payout destination is currently the SumUp Business Account. Ask whether a different destination, such as a bank account, is wanted.
  2. Check whether the owner's identity check (owner task 1) must be finished before a new payout destination can be added.
  3. Set up the method in the payout settings, under Profile, then Payouts. Check that the first payout arrives as expected.

Status: to do. The owner needs to decide which method to use.
