Administration — Staff Accounts

Admin Guide — Adding and Removing Staff

For the owner and the manager: how to add a staff member, change what they are allowed to do, reset a forgotten Personal Identification Number (PIN), and switch off a leaver without losing a single day's figures.

Who Should Do This Work

Only the owner or the manager. Do it at a quiet time, on a computer or tablet, in the SumUp web dashboard at sumup.me, where every setting sits on one screen and the register can carry on taking orders while you work. Counter staff do not need this guide.

User Names and Passwords Are on a Separate Page

The account logins are deliberately not written in this guide. They live on a separate page: open the offline copy of this documentation and scroll to User Passwords, or ask your engineer for the current list.

1. Your Team at a Glance

The installation created four profiles with individual PINs. Refunds and discounts are restricted to the manager profile, and every other profile can sell only.

2. Add a Staff Member

  1. Sign in to sumup.me as the owner. The verification code arrives on the owner's mobile phone, so have it to hand.
  2. Open the team / employee management area. Depending on the version it is labelled Employees, Team or Staff.
  3. Choose Add employee, or the plus button.
  4. Type the staff member's name. An e-mail address or position title is optional: add it only if you want it in the reports.
  5. Choose the permission level: staff for counter work, manager for refunds, discounts, reports and the close.
  6. Set the PIN: open the employee, choose PIN, switch on PIN access, then either let SumUp generate a code or type the one you have chosen.
  7. Save, then hand the PIN over privately — in person or by telephone, never in a group chat and never on a note stuck to the counter.
  8. Test it on the register: sign out, sign in with the new PIN, ring one cheap item, and confirm it appears under their name in the day's figures.

Menu Names Vary Between SumUp Versions

If a screen does not match the steps above, stop and ring 020 4520 9114 rather than guessing. A wrong toggle in this area can open refunds to everyone on the counter.

3. Change What Someone Can Do

Use this to promote a trusted counter worker to manager, or to take refund power away again after a role change.

  1. Open the person in the Employees screen.
  2. Change their permission level, or tick the individual permissions your version shows: refunds, discounts, sales history, reports.
  3. Save, then test it: sign in as them and confirm a restricted action is refused.

A Manager PIN Is a Refund PIN

Give the manager level only to someone you trust with the money, and review the list after every promotion, every leaver, and any incident you would rather not repeat.

4. Reset a Forgotten PIN

  1. Open the person in the Employees screen.
  2. Choose PIN and generate a new code, or type a new one.
  3. Tell them privately. The old code stops working at once.
  4. Never write the new code on the till, on any printed sheet, or in a photo on a phone.

Keep One Spare, Sealed

Keep a single spare manager PIN in a sealed envelope for the day someone forgets theirs mid-rush, and change it as soon as it has been used.

5. Remove a Staff Member

SumUp does not delete employee profiles, and that is a good thing: switching a profile off keeps every past sale attached to the right name, so the figures for earlier days stay correct.

  1. Do it on the day they leave, before their next shift could start.
  2. Open the person in the Employees screen.
  3. Switch their profile off (deactivate) — in current versions this is the toggle at the top of the screen.
  4. Check their PIN no longer unlocks the register.
  5. If they ever come back, switch the profile on again and issue them a fresh PIN.

Shared PIN Suspected? Reset, Do Not Deactivate

If you only suspect that a PIN is being lent out, change the PIN instead: it takes seconds, keeps the sales history intact, and the person keeps working. Deactivation is for leavers.

6. Security Rules That Keep the Figures Honest

  • One PIN per person: never lend it, never share it, never let two people work under one profile.
  • Give every new starter a fresh PIN on day one, and never recycle a leaver's code.
  • Only the owner knows the account password, and only the owner's phone receives the two-factor authentication (2FA) codes.
  • Refunds and discounts stay manager-only.
  • Read each staff member's sales total once a week: a name with far more sales than their shifts suggest usually means a shared PIN.

7. When to Ring the Engineer

Ring 020 4520 9114 before changing anything if a screen does not match this guide, if a PIN or a profile has vanished, or if anything to do with money looks wrong. Five minutes on the telephone beats a wrong toggle that opens refunds to the whole counter.
