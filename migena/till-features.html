EGGI'S — Till Feature Reference

What every feature of the till app does, and how far it has been checked on the device.

Print this page on A4. Each area starts on a new sheet. For step-by-step tasks, use the till procedures sheet instead.

How to read the status

  1. Seen opened on the device and described from the screen.
  2. Offer only an introduction and a "Get started" button. Not switched on for this account.
  3. Plus a POS Plus feature. Needs the paid plan.
  4. Web managed on the SumUp website, not on the till.
  5. Not confirmed not opened, or the screen did not finish loading.

1. Cart and checkout (main till screen)

  1. Category tabs — filters the item tiles by category. "All" shows everything. Seen
  2. Search (magnifier) — finds an item by name. Seen
  3. New item (pencil) — starts a new item from the till. Not confirmed
  4. Item tiles — tap to add the item to the cart. The price is shown on each tile. Seen
  5. Cart header — shows the order code, for example E-003, and the timing label. Seen
  6. Cart lines — one line per item, with quantity and price. Seen
  7. Add course — creates a course, so items can be sent to the kitchen in stages. Plus
  8. Park order — saves the order open and unpaid. Seen
  9. Card — opens the payment methods screen. Seen
  10. Chevron beside Card — opens the split and all-payment menu. Seen
  11. Three dots menu — manual price entry, add discount, add order note, clear cart. Seen
  12. Add order note — free text for the kitchen, up to 300 characters. Seen
  13. Clear cart — empties the cart. Only for a cart not yet sent. Seen
  14. Manual price entry — types in a price that is not on the menu. Seen (menu)
  15. Add discount — applies a discount to the cart. Seen (menu)
  16. Split — splits the bill between payments. Seen (menu)

2. Payment methods

  1. Tap to Pay — takes a contactless card or phone on the device's own NFC chip. Not available on this device (see section 13).
  2. Card Reader — sends the amount to the Solo card machine. Seen
  3. Payment Links — creates a link or QR code the customer pays online. Seen
Cash is not a payment method on this screen. Cash is counted into the till tray (see the till procedures, section 8.4).

3. Open orders (bell icon)

  1. Open order list — every parked order, with its code, time and items. Seen
  2. Search — finds an open order by code, item or amount. Seen
  3. New order — starts a new order. Seen
  4. Use courses banner — explains courses and links to POS Plus. Plus

4. Sales (receipt icon)

  1. Today's sales total — the total for the day, with a link to the detail. Seen
  2. Sales list by date — each sale with its items, time and result. Seen
  3. Search and filter — finds a sale; filters by date or result. Seen (search)
  4. Sale detail — the items, the VAT and the payment. Seen
  5. Receipt button — reprints the customer receipt. Seen
  6. Payment line — the card reference, time and result. Seen
A sale that did not complete shows "Unsuccessful" in red.

5. Reporting (chart icon)

  1. Periods — Today, Yesterday, This week, This month, Custom. Seen
  2. Sales total and count — the total and the number of sales. Seen
  3. Items — number of items sold in the period. Seen
  4. Average sale — total divided by the number of sales. Seen
  5. Refunds — money refunded in the period. Seen
  6. Discounts — money taken off by discounts. Seen
  7. Best-selling items — the items sold most. Seen
  8. Busiest period — sales by hour, and the busiest time. Seen
  9. Payment methods — how customers paid. Seen
  10. Insights, Revenue, Payouts, Fee invoice, Payments — detailed tabs. Seen (names)
  11. Go there now — opens the full reports on the website. Web
The figures show zero until a successful sale is made.

6. Items (price-tag icon)

  1. Library — the full list of items with their prices. Search, sort and filter. Seen
  2. Add item — creates a new item. Seen (button)
  3. Categories — the till categories and how many items each holds. Seen
  4. Inventory — stock tracking. Filter by low stock, sold out and tracked. Seen
  5. Modifiers — modifier sets and their choices, such as milk or bread. Seen
  6. Option sets — variations such as size or colour. Seen
  7. Deposits — container deposits, for example bottles. Plus
  8. Discounts — named discounts. The shop has 10%, 20% and 50%. Plus
  9. Create modifier set, option set or discount — creates a new group. Seen (buttons)
Clean-up needed: the modifier list still holds "(Example) Toppings", and the option sets still hold the sample "Color" and "Size" sets. The checklist already asks for the toppings set to be deleted.

7. Tools (grid icon)

The Tools screen is a menu. Each entry opens its own feature.

  1. Items — opens section 6. Seen
  2. Employees — staff accounts and their roles. Seen (section 8)
  3. Customers — customer list for invoices and loyalty. Seen (section 9)
  4. Reporting — opens section 5. Seen
  5. Online Store — builds a free online shop (Webspace). Offer only
  6. Bookings — appointment booking. Offer only
  7. Loyalty — reward programmes. Not confirmed (did not finish loading)
  8. Gift Cards — sells and redeems gift cards. Not confirmed
  9. Card readers — pairs and buys card machines. Not confirmed
  10. Tap to Pay — settings for contactless on the device. Seen (section 13)
  11. Payment links — creates customer payment links. Seen (section 2)
  12. Cash Advance — loans repaid from sales. Offer only The eligibility rules are shown: complete identity checks, take card payments for at least three months, and use the SumUp Business Account and card. Meeting them does not guarantee an offer.
  13. Invoices — creates and sends invoices. Not confirmed
  14. Expenses — tracks spending and exports it for tax. Offer only

8. Employees

  1. Members — lists everyone who can log in, with role and status. Seen
  2. Search, Role and Status filters — finds a member. Seen
  3. Add team member — invites a person by e-mail. Seen (button)
  4. Roles — the permission sets a member can have. Seen (tab)
  5. Edit team member — changes a member's role. Seen (panel)
On 19 November 2026 the only member is the account owner, as Administrator. The six staff e-mail addresses are still needed (see the checklist). Each staff member needs their own address to be invited.

9. Customers

  1. Customer list — names and contact details of customers. Seen (empty)
  2. Add customer — adds a customer by hand. Seen (button)
  3. Invoice customers note — invoice customers are kept in the Invoices directory, not on this list. Seen
The list is empty. Customers are added by hand, or created from invoices.

10. Profile (person icon, bottom left)

  1. Account name and merchant code — shows MW82CKEA. Seen
  2. 24/7 priority support — opens support. Seen
  3. Inbox — messages from SumUp, with an unread count. Seen
  4. Login and security — password and two-factor authentication. Seen (name)
  5. Payouts — money sent to the bank account. Seen
  6. Payout settings — which bank account receives payouts. Seen (name)
  7. Tap to Pay — see section 13. Seen
  8. Card Reader — card machine settings. Seen (name)

11. Settings and business profile (web)

These are on the website, not the till.

  1. Business information — registered name, trade name, address. Web
  2. Business profile — name on SumUp listings, statement descriptor and phone. Web
  3. VAT — VAT rates charged. Web
  4. Plans and fees — the current plan and its fees. Web
  5. Employees — staff profiles (also on the till). Web
The registered name still reads "Greens Kitchen". Only SumUp Support can change it (checklist, section 8.5).

12. Printing and kitchen (checklist)

The printing and order-sending settings are per device and are not in the till's menu. They are covered by checklist Rules U3 and U4:

  1. The "Till" printer prints POS Lite receipts.
  2. The Solo's counter printer prints standalone card receipts.
  3. The Terminal prints order tickets only where "Print order tickets" is on.

13. Known limits on this device

  1. Tap to Pay: not available. The device reports "NFC not available", so contactless payment on this device will not work. Use the Solo.
  2. Card readers and Loyalty did not finish loading during this check, so they are not confirmed.
  3. Refunds and split bills have not been used on a real sale.
