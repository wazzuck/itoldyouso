# EGGI'S — Till Procedures (front of house)

**Summary:** this sheet shows how to open the till, take a table order,
record allergies, take payment, handle refunds and close the day.

Print this document on A4, one section per page.

---

## Who this is for

1. Every member of front-of-house staff.
2. The manager, who also has the refund and close-down steps.

## Words you will see on the screen

1. **POS** — Point of Sale. The till app on the Chromebook.
2. **Cart** — the list of items for the table you are serving now.
3. **Park order** — save an order to come back to later. Nothing is paid.
4. **Order note** — a free-text message for the kitchen, such as an
   allergy.
5. **PIN** — personal identification number, your four-digit login.
6. **Solo** — the card machine that takes the customer's payment.

## Before you start

1. The Chromebook is on and the POS app is open.
2. The card machine (Solo) shows a green or charged screen.
3. Your PIN is known to you only. Never share it.

---

## 1. Opening the till (first person in, about 10 minutes)

1. Switch on the Chromebook and open the **POS** app.
2. Log in with your **PIN**.
3. Check the top bar shows the shop name and no warning banner.
4. Check the printer has paper. If not, load a roll (section 13).
5. Count the cash float into the till tray. Write the total on the
   cash sheet and have a second person check it.
6. Check the kitchen device is showing the open order list.

**If a warning banner appears:** note its words, then go to section 14.

---

## 2. Logging in and out

1. Tap your name or the login prompt.
2. Enter your PIN.
3. When your shift ends, log out. The account icon (bottom left) is
   where to look; the exact **Log out** label is not yet confirmed
   (section 15). Never leave the till logged in and unattended.

---

## 3. Taking a table order

1. Tap **New order** on the **Open orders** screen (the bell icon on
   the left bar), or tap the cart icon to start a new cart.
2. Choose the menu category along the top, such as **03 Brunch & Beyond**.
   Tap **All** to see everything.
3. Tap an item to add it to the cart.
4. Check the cart on the right. Each line shows the quantity and price.
5. Repeat for every item the table has ordered.
6. Tap **Park order** if the table is still deciding. The button that
   sends the order to the kitchen is not yet confirmed (section 15).
7. Confirm the order appears on the kitchen display (section 9).

---

## 4. Modifiers (choices such as milk or bread)

Modifiers are options attached to an item. The till asks for them
after you tap the item.

1. Tap the item.
2. Choices are expected to appear, such as **Milk** (oat, almond,
   soya, coconut) or **Bread**. The exact prompt is not yet confirmed
   on the device (section 15); stop and ask the manager if it differs.
3. Tap the customer's choice and confirm it.
4. Check the modifier is written under the item name in the cart.

**Common modifier groups in this shop:**

1. **Drinks:** milk choice, extra shot, decaf, syrups.
2. **Food:** bread, egg style, eggs benedict choice, omelette fillings,
   pasta type, jacket potato filling.

**If a modifier is missing:** do not guess. Add the item with an order
note (section 5) saying what the customer wants.

---

## 5. Allergies and special requests

**Rule 1: every allergy goes in the order note.** A modifier is for
choices on the menu; the order note is for anything else, including
allergies.

**Rule 2: say it aloud to the kitchen.** The order note is a written
message, so a second check by voice is still needed.

**How to add an order note:**

1. Tap the three dots at the top right of the cart.
2. Tap **Add order note**.
3. Type the allergy in plain words, for example:
   `NUT ALLERGY — no nuts, no nut oil, check the sauce`.
4. Tap **Save**. The note is saved with the order.
5. Send the order, then tell the kitchen in person.

**What to write:** the allergen, the item it affects, and the
customer's table number. Keep it under 300 characters, the limit
the till allows.

**What not to do:** never write only "allergy" with no detail, and
never remove the note by accident. If the customer changes their
mind, edit the note rather than deleting it.

---

## 6. Changing or cancelling items

1. **Clear the whole cart:** tap the three dots, then **Clear cart**.
   Use this only for a cart that has not been sent to the kitchen.
2. **Change one item:** remove it, then add the correct one.
3. **Item already sent to the kitchen:** tell the manager. Do not
   cancel it on the till without them.

---

## 7. Discounts and manual prices

These are manager-only.

1. Tap the three dots, then **Add discount**, or **Manual price entry**.
2. Enter the amount or percentage, and the reason.
3. The manager enters their PIN to confirm.

---

## 8. Taking payment

**Rule: the customer pays after the food is served, unless the
manager has agreed otherwise.**

### 8.1 Card (Solo)

1. Tap **Card** at the bottom right of the cart.
2. Choose **Card Reader**. The payment amount appears on the Solo.
3. Hand the Solo to the customer. They tap, insert or pay with their
   phone.
4. Wait for the green tick and the printed receipt.
5. Give the customer the receipt.

### 8.2 Tap to pay (phone)

1. Tap **Card**, then **Tap to Pay**.
2. The customer holds their card or phone on the Chromebook or the
   Solo, as the screen shows.

### 8.3 Payment link

1. Tap **Card**, then **Payment Links**.
2. Show the link or QR code to the customer.

### 8.4 Cash

1. Cash is not taken on the payment screen. Count it into the till
   tray, then give the change.
2. Record the cash in the cash sheet.
3. Keep the drawer closed when not in use.

### 8.5 Split bills

Split bills are not yet tested on this till. Ask the manager before
using them. (Section 15.)

---

## 9. The kitchen

The client has asked that one SumUp Terminal is the kitchen Terminal.
It shows the open orders and prints a ticket for each parked order.

1. Every parked order appears on the kitchen Terminal within seconds.
2. The kitchen Terminal prints one ticket per parked order.
3. Tell the kitchen about every allergy aloud, as well as the order
   note.

**The kitchen display system is not yet decided.** When it is
chosen, this section will be replaced.

---

## 10. Refunds (manager only)

**Refunds are not yet tested on this till.** The steps below must be
confirmed on a real test sale before use. (Section 15.)

1. Open **Sales** (receipt icon, left bar).
2. Find the sale and tap it.
3. Tap the refund option, if present.
4. Enter the manager PIN.
5. Print the refund receipt and file it with the day's paperwork.

---

## 11. Reprinting a receipt

1. Open **Sales** (receipt icon, left bar).
2. Tap the sale.
3. Tap **Receipt**.

---

## 12. Closing the till (last person, about 20 minutes)

1. Check there are no open orders on the **Open orders** screen.
   Pay or clear each one with the manager.
2. Open **Reporting** (chart icon, left bar) and check **Today's sales**.
3. Count the cash in the tray and compare it with the sales total.
   Write the difference on the cash sheet.
4. Log out, then switch the Chromebook off.
5. Lock the cash in the safe or lockable tray.

**The end-of-day Z-report** is not yet tested on this till. Ask the
manager. (Section 15.)

---

## 13. Paper and printer

1. Open the printer lid.
2. Drop in a new roll so the paper feeds from the bottom.
3. Close the lid. Check a test receipt prints.

---

## 14. When something goes wrong

1. **The till freezes:** wait 30 seconds, then close and reopen the
   POS app. Your open orders are saved.
2. **Card declined:** ask the customer to try another card or method.
   Do not retry more than twice.
3. **No internet:** the Solo can still take card payments on its own
   connection. Tell the manager.
4. **Printer will not print:** check the paper and the lid, then tell
   the manager.
5. **Anything else:** call the manager. Do not guess.
