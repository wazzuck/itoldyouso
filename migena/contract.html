    Service Contract & Scope of Work — SumUp Till Delivery

    1. Agreement Parties & Purpose

    Service Provider: IToldYouSo Computing — Neville Bryce, Senior Software Engineer (Director)
    Address: Corringham Court, Corringham Road, London NW11 7BY
    Client: Migena Lika (Primary Contact) & Egzon Quilia ('the Client') — 345 Green Lanes, London N13 4JG
    ASAP before Target Launch Date: Sunday 22nd November 2026 (Rapid turnaround deployment)

    Core Commitment: I will deliver a complete, turn-key Electronic Point of Sale (EPOS) installation for your cafe and brunch spot ahead of your opening on Sunday 22nd November 2026:

    • Register & Card Reader Setup: Configure your SumUp 13-inch Full HD countertop touchscreen register and companion SumUp Solo handheld card reader.
    • Dual-Printer & Cash Drawer Integration: Pair your front-of-house checkout printer and kitchen order ticket printer, and verify cash drawer solenoid compatibility.
    • Complete Menu, Modifiers & Gratuity Programming: Programme your full item catalogue (10–20 brunch items, 10–20 lunch items, London cafe coffees with modifiers, soft drinks, beers, and wine), including customer gratuity/tipping prompts, eat-in vs takeaway tags, and receipt branding.
    • Staff Accounts & Sales Attribution: Set up 4 individual staff PIN accounts with separate sales tracking.
    • Manager Handover Training (Single Session): Provide unhurried 1-on-1 handover training in 1 single session for the manager (who will then train the rest of the staff) so the team feels 100% confident taking card and cash payments during morning coffee and lunchtime rushes.
    • Post-Installation Website Documentation: Release custom operational documentation on this website upon completion — factoring in findings from the installation and requests from the owner — free for you to view, print, or download for later reproduction.

    2. Pre-Flight Checks

    On the day of installation at your North London cafe, I start with the following pre-flight checks to verify the network and counter environment before mounting and configuring your till equipment:

    Network Resilience & Cafe Environment Isolation

    • ☐ Check 5GHz Wi-Fi Service Set Identifier (SSID) signal strength and routing to separate cafe till traffic and card readers from visiting customer guest devices.

    3. Day of Installation Protocol

    On the day of installation, I manage the physical and digital setup end-to-end:

    • ☐ Unpack and mount hardware on your counter / service area with tidy, concealed cable management away from liquids.
    • ☐ Connect the till to the dedicated cafe broadband connection.
    • ☐ Pair your SumUp Solo handheld card reader with the 13-inch countertop register.
    • ☐ Configure your food and drink menu, categories (10–20 brunch items, 10–20 lunch items, artisan coffee, teas, soft drinks, beers, and wine), item modifiers (oat milk, almond milk, soya milk, extra shot, decaf, syrups), customer gratuity/tipping prompts, receipt header/footer details, and eat-in vs takeaway tax tags.
    • ☐ Configure 4 individual staff Personal Identification Number (PIN) accounts with separate sales attribution and shift reporting.
    • ☐ Run end-to-end test transactions: card tap, phone pay, chip and PIN, cash change, split bills, and refunds.

    4. Manager Handover Training Syllabus (Single Session)

    Technology in a busy cafe must never cause stress during a rush. I deliver a calm, unhurried 1-on-1 handover training session with the manager in 1 single session, equipping the manager to operate the till confidently and train the rest of the staff:

    • Morning Opening Routine: Powering up the terminal, checking network status, verifying printer paper, and opening the cash float.
    • Ringing Up Food & Drinks: Selecting hot beverages, applying drink modifiers (milks, shots, syrups), assigning table numbers or takeaway bags, and fast counter queue busting.
    • Card Payments & Gratuity: Handing off to the customer, prompting for tips/gratuity, waiting for the green confirmation chime, and handling declined cards gracefully.
    • Cash Handling: Calculating exact change and securing the cash drawer.
    • Split Bills & Custom Orders: Splitting brunch checks across multiple cards or combining cash and card payments.
    • Evening Close-Out (Z-Reports): Cashing up, reconciling card totals against merchant payouts, and preparing the till for the next morning.
    • Post-Installation Website Documentation & Cheat Sheet: Custom operational documentation (Cafe Client Handbook and Countertop Quick Cheat Sheet) will be released on this website at completion, factoring in findings from the installation and specific requests from the owner. You are free to view, print, or download it for later reproduction across your team.
